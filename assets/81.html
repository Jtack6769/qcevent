Tonight’s Feature: Logan’s Run

Our grand 8.0 headliner — the adventure that started with the 1976 film and ran right into the 1978 TV series.

EXPERIENCE THE ADVENTURE! * THE TALES OF LOGAN’S RUN (1976) *

The Story

In the 23rd century, everyone lives inside the City of Domes — and nobody lives past 30. At Carousel, your time is up. Logan 5 is a Sandman, a hunter of Runners who try to escape. Then he starts asking questions, and with Jessica 6 he runs for the one place that might not exist at all: Sanctuary.

The 1978 TV Series

The CBS series ran 1977–78 for one season of 14 episodes. Gregory Harrison played Logan 5, Heather Menzies played Jessica 6, and Donald Moffat joined as Rem, an android created just for television. The last episodes aired in early 1978 — “Futurepast,” “Carousel,” “Night Visitors,” “Turnabout,” and the finale, “Stargate,” on February 6, 1978.

A Star Trek Connection

Captain, this one’s for you: D. C. Fontana was story editor on the series, working alongside other Star Trek writers. David Gerrold wrote “Man Out of Time,” and Harlan Ellison wrote a treatment for “Crypt.”
