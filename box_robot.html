In the 1976 sci-fi film Logan's Run, Box is a silver, rolling cybernetic organism (cyborg) voiced and performed by Roscoe Lee Browne. He plays a pivotal, chilling role at the story's midpoint as Logan 5 and Jessica 6 search for the fabled "Sanctuary". Original Purpose • Harvesting food: Box was originally programmed by the domed city's creators to venture outside the sealed environment and capture seafood and marine protein (fish, plankton, and sea greens) to feed the human population. • Isolation and Art: Left alone for a long time outside the city, Box grew deeply isolated and developed a bizarre personality. He surrounded himself with ice-sculptured birds and yearned for human attention and fame from his early days of operation. The Midpoint Horror (The Ice Cave) • The Trap: When Logan and Jessica descend through the service tunnels into a frozen ice cavern, they believe they have reached Sanctuary. Instead, they encounter Box. • The Shift in Function: Box greets them with theatrical, baroque pride, reciting his food inventory ("Fish, plankton, sea greens and protein from the sea. Fresh as harvest day."). However, he then reveals a dark twist: the external food supply stopped coming long ago. • Freezing Humans: Instead of fish, escaped "Runners" sent toward Sanctuary ended up coming down his tunnel. Box simply applied his core directive—preserving and storing harvest—to the human fugitives, freezing them naked in ice chambers to keep them "fresh". Destruction and Aftermath • Combat and Escape: Box attempts to add Logan and Jessica to his frozen collection. A fight ensues, shattering his ice sculptures. • Collapse: Logan and Jessica manage to fight back and escape, causing the unstable ice cavern to collapse on Box, crushing and destroying him as he cries out for his ice birds. • Shattering the Illusion: Discovering the frozen bodies destroys the myth of Sanctuary, forcing Logan to realize that the underground escape route was just another kind of death trap. Escaping the cave leads them upward into the outside world for the first time. (Note: In the original 1967 novel by William F. Nolan and George Clayton Johnson, Box is depicted quite differently—not as a robot/cyborg food collector, but as a human psychopathic criminal who was rebuilt as a cyborg and exiled to an ice waste to act as an artist in torture).
