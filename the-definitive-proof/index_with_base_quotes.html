📜 Rules of Engagement

  • First quote: same opening quote for both players. First correct answer earns 25 points and goes first.
  • Each round: 1 point for show/movie + 1 point for speaker.
  • Surrounding line: +1 for the line before or after.
  • Defense: waiting player can hit I HAVE IT before the active player answers. Correct steal +1; wrong steal −1.
  • Captain's Chair Bonus: after 5 or 10 rounds, the losing player can initiate it only when they still have a mathematical chance.
  • Bonus: 3 choices. 3 questions after 5 rounds or 6 after 10. Each correct answer = 10 points.
